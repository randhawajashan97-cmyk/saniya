# Luxury Salon & Academy Website

## Goal
Create a polished, single-page website for **Luxury Salon & Academy** that turns visitors into appointment enquiries, while also giving the academy a clear supporting presence.

## Page experience
- Build a full-width editorial opening with original luxury beauty imagery, the business name as the main headline, a prominent **Book an appointment** action, and the 4.8 rating from 36 reviews.
- Add a refined salon introduction followed by service-category highlights. Keep the wording broad and omit unprovided prices or unsupported claims.
- Present the academy as a distinct section for prospective learners, with an enquiry action rather than invented course details.
- Include an image-led work/gallery section using a cohesive set of generated salon, styling, makeup, and academy visuals.
- Add a reviews area that references the supplied aggregate rating without fabricating customer quotes.
- Finish with a contact and visit section showing the supplied address, opening status context, phone number, website, and map/directions links.
- Use a compact sticky navigation that smoothly moves between the one-page sections, plus a strong mobile call-to-book action.

## Visual direction
- Use the selected **Editorial Luxe** palette: ivory `#F7F3ED`, near-black `#171717`, muted gold `#AD8B54`, and white `#FFFFFF`, translated into reusable theme colors.
- Pair **Instrument Serif** headings with **Work Sans** body text.
- Favor magazine-like image crops, generous whitespace, thin rules, sharp alignment, restrained 0–8px corners, and minimal gold detailing.
- Add subtle image reveals and text transitions, with reduced-motion support; avoid flashy effects and generic beauty-template styling.
- Generate a small, cohesive image set featuring polished hair, makeup, beauty-service detail, and academy training scenes. Keep generated imagery free of logos and text.

## Appointment interactions
- Make the primary booking action open a direct phone call to `076269 51020`.
- Provide secondary website and directions actions using the supplied business details.
- Ensure all navigation, phone, website, and directions actions work on desktop and mobile.

## Technical details

- Add unique home-page metadata for Luxury Salon & Academy, including title, description, Open Graph fields, and Twitter card information.

## Content assumptions
